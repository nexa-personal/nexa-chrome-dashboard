# NEXA Privacy

NEXA stores dashboard settings, tasks, custom bookmarks, world-clock cities, selected markets, RSS feed addresses, widget sizes, cached market data custom wallpaper and photo-frame pictures locally in Chrome extension storage.

NEXA does not sell, rent, or transmit this stored user data to a developer-owned server. There is no developer-owned server and no analytics or tracking.

NEXA contacts the following third-party services directly from your browser, only to show the data you asked for: Open-Meteo (weather, air quality, city search), ipwho.is / ipify (your public IP for the Network widget), CoinGecko (crypto prices), Frankfurter (forex rates), TGJU (Iranian market prices), Google Favicon service (bookmark icons), and the RSS feeds you add (including the default Google News feed). These requests contain only what the service needs (e.g. coordinates for weather, asset names for prices).

Location is used only when you press the location button in the weather widget. NEXA stores the last chosen weather location locally and does not keep location history.

The Network widget measures ping by sending small requests from your browser to the targets you choose (defaults: Cloudflare and Google). Photos you add to the photo frame never leave your device.
